The Monograph

A Story of Food, Roots and Everyday Goodness.

In traditional Indian households, the Chhoti Tokri—or little basket—was never merely a container. It was a daily ritual of care. Early each morning, fresh greens, sacred grains, and potent spices were gathered thoughtfully in small proportions, ensuring nothing was wasted and everything was savored at its prime vitality.

Today, Chhoti Tokri reimagines this sacred rhythm for a global pantry. We honor the custodians of ancestral seeds, bridging remote farmsteads with refined culinary tables from New Delhi to Paris.

Provenance Protocol

Thoughtfully Sourced. Carefully Selected.

We prioritize transparency through verified geographical origin, direct relationships with multi-generational cultivators, and rigorous physical batch inspection—honoring true agricultural lineage.

01

Origin Traceability

Every harvest lot is tagged directly to specific micro-regions, recording indigenous soil characteristics, elevation, and village provenance down to individual cooperative groves.

02

Seasonal Harvesting

Gathered strictly during natural maturity windows, avoiding force-ripening or premature harvesting to ensure authentic flavor density and optimal aromatic terpene profiles.

03

Gentle Preservation

Preserving natural phytonutrients through low-temperature solar drying, mechanical cold-pressing, and hermetic micro-lot sealing without synthetic stabilizing agents.
