ORIGIN & HERITAGE

A Rice with a Distinctive Heritage

Grown along the fertile plains of eastern Uttar Pradesh, particularly in and around the Siddharthnagar region, Kala Namak rice carries generations of agricultural heritage. Known for its dark, inky husk enclosing a slender pale grain, this cherished variety has long been treasured in family kitchens and festive meals across north India.

Unlike high-yield modern varieties, Kala Namak is cultivated with time-honoured agrarian methods suited to the region's climate. When simmered, it releases a distinctive natural scent and stays soft and separate, making it one of India's most celebrated heritage grains.

CULINARY INSPIRATION

How to Enjoy

Upon cooking, Kala Namak grains become slender, fluffy, and separate. Its delicate texture and pleasant aroma make it a wonderful centerpiece for both quiet daily meals and special festive occasions.

Steamed with Ghee

Cook simply in fresh water and serve warm with a spoonful of pure desi cow ghee to highlight the rice's natural aroma and subtle sweetness.

Pulao & Khichdi

Ideal for festive pulaos, subtle biryanis, and wholesome khichdi where individual fluffy grains and mild spices can shine.

With Regional Dals & Curries

Pairs wonderfully with aromatic yellow tadka dal, slow-simmered chana masala, or rich regional vegetable curries.
