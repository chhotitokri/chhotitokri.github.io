# Content strategy — India organic search

## Purpose

Build topical authority around **verified** Indian pantry products and brand trust. No checkout on site; goal is discovery, education, and enquiry.

## Pillars

1. **Product education** — one indexable URL per catalogue SKU (implemented).
2. **Tokri Journal** — long-form articles (3 live; expand per calendar).
3. **Sourcing transparency** — `/our-sourcing` and `/about`.
4. **Conversion without purchase** — `/contact`, trade modal, mailto.
