## Brand & Style

The design system embodies quiet luxury, botanical provenance, and curatorial reverence for artisanal Indian culinary traditions. Designed as an international showcase for discerning global partners—importers, Michelin-starred culinary teams, and luxury specialty distributors across Europe and the globe—the aesthetic rejects commercial transactional tropes entirely. There are no price tags, shopping carts, or retail triggers. Instead, the UI reads as an archival monograph and contemporary food publication.

### Style Directives
- **Editorial Archival:** High-contrast serif headlines paired with airy, deliberate compositions evoke fine hardcover art books and French culinary periodicals.
- **Botanical Restraint:** The palette is anchored by deep foliage greens, warm parchment ivory, and delicate muted gold hairlines that frame content without ostentation.
- **Tactile Sensibility:** Flat surfaces with warm undertones and crisp paper-like cards establish depth through structural proportion rather than heavy drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in agricultural heritage and natural materials:

- **Primary (`#174D32` - Forest Green):** Represents ancient groves, botanical origin, and foundational authority. Used for primary narrative moments, solid editorial badges, and structural emphasis.
- **Secondary (`#B88A3B` - Muted Antique Gold):** Evokes raw unrefined brass, turmeric threads, and gilded manuscript margins. Reserved for meticulous hairline rules, accent insignias, and curation metadata.
- **Tertiary / Container (`#EADFC9` - Earthy Beige):** Grounding canvas tone reminiscent of sun-bleached jute, unrefined grain, and limestone cellars. Applied to architectural section bands and subtle framed panels.
- **Background (`#F8F5EC` - Warm Ivory):** The primary canvas. Provides warmth and depth far superior to stark sterile white, softening typography for long-form reading.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1A241E` - Botanical Charcoal):** A deep evergreen charcoal used for all reading text, structural frames, and iconography. Replaces pure black to avoid harsh digital contrast.
- **Neutral Light (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp White):** Utilized selectively for archival artifact cards and high-focus content panels to lift them cleanly above the warm ivory canvas.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary weight of Playfair Display with the refined clarity of Plus Jakarta Sans.

- **Headlines & Titles:** Set in Playfair Display. Used for product titles, provenance narratives, harvest essays, and thematic volume introductions. Italicized cuts are leveraged for French tasting notes, regional origins (e.g., *Malabar Coast*, *Kashmir Valley*), and botanical classifications.
- **Body:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides crisp legibility at generous line heights, facilitating uninterrupted reading of terroir notes, artisanal methods, and trade specifications.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** All labels are set in uppercase Plus Jakarta Sans with wide tracking (`0.12em` to `0.16em`) to echo luxury specimen indexing and museum archival cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors fine press books and architectural portfolios, relying on asymmetric white space and rigid alignment rather than dense information stacking.

### Layout Model
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid grid bound to a maximum width of 1440px. Outer margins are expansive (`4rem`), allowing imagery and copy to breathe. Columns alternate between generous single-story spans (8 columns for narratives, 4 columns for spec summaries).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standardized 2.5rem margin with adaptive reflow for split panels.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Tightened 1.25rem margins with strictly single-column linear progression to maintain the cadence of an illuminated manuscript.

### Spacing Cadence
- Content sections are spaced generously using `space-xl` and higher multiples to cleanly separate distinct terroir stories.
- Elements within cards maintain airy internal padding (minimum `space-lg`) to prevent photographic assets from competing with text.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain the quiet luxury and editorial presence, the design system avoids diffuse drop shadows and heavy elevation planes entirely.

- **Structural Borders & Hairlines:** Depth is established through crisp `1px` borders in `#EADFC9` or `#B88A3B` at 40% opacity. Surfaces feel like archival paper sheets laid flat upon a curator's desk.
- **Tonal Layering:** Visual hierarchy is built through solid, tonal contrast: `#FFFFFF` panels resting over the `#F8F5EC` canvas, anchored by occasional `#EADFC9` framing mats.
- **Subtle Surface Lift:** For interactive cards and media panels, hover states introduce a micro-translation (`translateY(-2px)`) accompanied by an ethereal, low-contrast shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(26, 36, 30, 0.06)`. Heavy blurs, glowing halos, or neomorphic contours are strictly forbidden.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts Soft geometry (`roundedness: 1`), utilizing minimal 4px corner radii (`0.25rem`) on cards, imagery, and inputs. 

This restrained curvature softens the digital edge without compromising the tailored, architectural feel of traditional bookbinding and printed museum catalogues. Structural pill shapes are reserved exclusively for circular harvest indicators and botanical seal badges.

## Components

### Buttons & Curatorial Inquiries
- **Primary Inquiry Action:** A solid `#174D32` container, crisp typography in `#F8F5EC`, subtle 4px corner radius. No gradient. On hover, transitions smoothly to a deep botanical tone `#113824` with a gentle gold border reveal (`#B88A3B`). Used for "Request Dossier", "Initiate Partnership", or "Private Tasting Request".
- **Secondary / Dossier Link:** Transparent background with a `1px` hairline border in `#B88A3B` and text in `#174D32`. Uppercase label styling with tracked letter spacing.
- **Text Link:** Understated `#1A241E` with an offset hairline rule in `#B88A3B` that smoothly expands from center on hover.

### Artifact & Heritage Cards
- **Construction:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a `1px` stroke of `#EADFC9`. Generous internal padding (`space-lg`).
- **Imagery:** Hero imagery occupies uncropped, editorial ratios (3:4 or 16:10) with warm desaturation, framed by a delicate hairline inset.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Provenance eyebrow label (e.g., *WAYANAD, KERALA • MONSOON HARVEST*) in `#B88A3B`, product name in Playfair Display, and a concise botanical synopsis. Explicitly devoid of commerce controls, ratings, or add-to-cart buttons.

### Chips & Provenance Badges
- **Specification Badges:** Flat `#F8F5EC` background with a subtle border in `#EADFC9`, displaying text in `#1A241E` using `label-md`. Used for markers such as *GI Tagged*, *Single Estate*, *Organic Certified*, and *AOP Equivalent*.
- **Botanical Seal:** Circular emblem with `#174D32` typography centered inside a fine `#B88A3B` perimeter.

### Lists & Technical Specifications
- **Catalogue Spec Tables:** Divided strictly by `1px` horizontal rules in `#EADFC9`. Left column holds parameter labels (e.g., *Terroir*, *Moisture Content*, *Packaging Format*) in `label-md` muted botanical charcoal; right column holds descriptive editorial responses in `body-md`.

### Form Controls & Partnership Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Minimalist styling with a light `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F5EC` ground, a defined `1px` outline in `#EADFC9`, and text in `#1A241E`. Focus states activate a crisp `#174D32` outline with an antique gold halo ring at 20% opacity.
- **Selection Checks:** Minimal square boxes with 2px radius. Active state fills with `#174D32` and presents a custom antique gold check mark.

### Archival Folio Dividers
- Full-width micro-hairlines in `#EADFC9` interrupted centrally by a small diamond motif or harvest year in `#B88A3B` tracking.
