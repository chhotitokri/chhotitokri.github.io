HERITAGE ARCHIVAL MONOGRAPH•Chambal Valley & Rajasthan

Traditional Mustard Oil

In the arid, sun-baked landscape of the Chambal Valley and eastern Rajasthan, heirloom Brassica juncea seeds have been cultivated for centuries. Our mustard oil is pressed through traditional wooden rotary expellers (Kohlu) at cold temperatures under 40°C. This gentle physical method retains the precious natural allyl isothiocyanate compound that gives the oil its legendary pungency and golden viscosity.

ORIGIN & HERITAGE

A Harvest with a Distinctive Heritage

Grown in traditional agricultural belts and nurtured through natural weather cycles, Traditional Mustard Oil reflects centuries of indigenous agricultural mastery. Each lot is gathered in close coordination with multigenerational farming families who preserve native seed biodiversity.

Unlike mass commodity agriculture, no chemical ripening agents or artificial polishing are employed. The natural textures, volatile essential compounds, and rich aromas are shielded through patient low-temperature handling and careful hermetic packaging.
